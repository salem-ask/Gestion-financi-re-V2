Inside the book

10 chapters, a progression designed to be read start to finish

01 The Foundations

What a biblical symbol is, why the Bible speaks in symbols, how to interpret it according to context, the continuity between Old and New Testament, and the traps to avoid from the start.

02 Elements of Nature

Water, fire, wind and breath, rain and dew, light and darkness: the most frequent symbolic vocabulary in the whole Bible, finally explained.

03 Symbolic Places

Mountain, desert, sea, sky, Eden, Egypt, Babylon, Jerusalem, temple, New Jerusalem: biblical geography as theological language.

04 Symbolic Animals

Lamb, lion, serpent, eagle, dove, fish, ox, horse, sheep, goat, raven: why the same animal can carry very different meanings depending on the passage.

05 Biblical Colors

White, red and scarlet, blue, purple, black, green, gold, silver: what each color meant to the Bible's first readers.

06 Sacred Objects and Elements

Oil, bread, wine, blood, salt, incense, lampstand, ark, altar, veil, stone, crown, staff, sword, garments: the meaning of worship and its objects.

07 Sacred Numbers

3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 40, 70 — and 666 covered in depth, with two readings (historical and symbolic), with no calendar speculation or date-calculating whatsoever.

08 Symbolic Gestures and Actions

Foot washing, the laying on of hands, fasting, baptism, kneeling, raising hands, the prophets' acted-out prophetic signs.

09 Prophetic and Apocalyptic Symbols

The four living creatures, the four horsemen, the beast, the dragon, the woman, the whore of Babylon, the new heaven and the new earth.

10 Jesus and the Kingdom of God Through Symbols

The Lamb, the Lion, the light of the world, the bread of life, the door, the good shepherd, the true vine, the body of Christ, the bride, the Kingdom: the convergence of every symbol onto one person.

The book closes with a methodological conclusion and 5 appendices: an alphabetical dictionary of about sixty symbols, a summary table, the most frequent symbols, symbols whose meaning varies by context, and a Bible-reference index sorted by book.
